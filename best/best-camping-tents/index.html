Frequently asked questions

Do I need a footprint?

Optional, but it protects the floor and adds life to lightweight tents.

What is a 3-season tent?

Built for spring, summer and fall. Not intended for heavy snow or extreme winds.

How much should a backpacking tent weigh?

Under about 2 kg for two people is a good target; ultralight tents go well below that.
