Frequently asked questions

What type of hosting do I need?

Shared hosting suits new sites; managed WordPress suits growing blogs and stores; cloud or VPS suits high traffic or custom apps.

Why do renewal prices matter?

Introductory prices can double or triple at renewal. Compare 3-year total cost, not month one.

Can I host for free?

Static sites can host free on GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages or Netlify.
