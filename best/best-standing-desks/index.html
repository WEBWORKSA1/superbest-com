Frequently asked questions

How long should I stand?

Alternate often. Many ergonomists suggest changing position every 30–60 minutes rather than standing all day.

One motor or two?

Dual motors lift more weight, move faster and usually last longer.

What height range do I need?

Your elbows should sit at about 90° while typing, both sitting and standing.
