Frequently asked questions

How often should I replace running shoes?

Typically every 500–800 km, or when cushioning feels flat.

Do I need stability shoes?

Only if you want extra support. Many runners do fine in neutral shoes; comfort is the best guide.

Should I size up?

Leave about a thumb's width in front of your longest toe; feet swell on longer runs.
