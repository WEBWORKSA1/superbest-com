Frequently asked questions

Is an air fryer healthier?

It generally uses far less oil than deep frying, which can cut fat and calories for fried-style foods.

What size should I buy?

2–4 qt suits one or two people; 5–6 qt suits families; dual-basket or oven styles suit bigger households.

Can I use foil or parchment?

Yes, if weighted by food and not blocking airflow. Never preheat with loose liners inside.
