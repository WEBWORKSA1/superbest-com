Frequently asked questions

Is noise cancelling bad for your ears?

No. ANC lets you listen at lower volumes in loud places, which can reduce overall exposure. Keep volume moderate and take breaks.

Over-ear or earbuds for noise cancelling?

Over-ear models usually block more noise and last longer per charge; earbuds win on portability.

Do codecs matter?

For most listeners on phones, AAC or aptX is plenty. LDAC helps only with high-resolution sources on compatible Android phones.
