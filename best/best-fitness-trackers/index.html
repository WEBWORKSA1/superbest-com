Frequently asked questions

Which is more accurate: wrist or ring?

Both use optical sensors. Rings often do well for sleep and resting heart rate; wrist devices are better for workouts and GPS.

Do I need built-in GPS?

Only if you want accurate pace and distance without carrying your phone.

Are subscriptions required?

Some brands lock advanced insights behind a monthly fee. Check the total cost over two years.
