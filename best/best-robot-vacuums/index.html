Frequently asked questions

Are robot vacuums worth it?

For daily maintenance cleaning, yes. They keep floors consistently tidy; you will still deep-clean occasionally.

Do I need obstacle avoidance?

If you have pets, kids or cables on the floor, camera-based avoidance is worth paying for.

How often do I replace parts?

Typically filters every 2–3 months, side brushes every 3–6 months and main brushes every 6–12 months.
