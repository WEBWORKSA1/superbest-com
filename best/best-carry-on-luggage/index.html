Frequently asked questions

What size is a carry-on?

Many US airlines allow about 22 × 14 × 9 inches including wheels. International and budget airlines are often smaller, so check before you fly.

Hardside or softside?

Hardside protects contents and resists weather; softside flexes into tight bins and adds pockets.

Is a lifetime warranty worth it?

For frequent flyers, yes. Read what it covers, because airline damage is sometimes excluded.
