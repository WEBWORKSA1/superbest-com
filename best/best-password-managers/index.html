Frequently asked questions

Are password managers safe?

Reputable ones encrypt your vault on your device, so the company cannot read it. Use a strong master password and turn on two-factor authentication.

Free or paid?

Bitwarden and Proton Pass free plans cover most people. Paid plans add sharing, family vaults and extras.

What about passkeys?

Passkeys replace passwords with phishing-resistant keys. All our picks can store and sync them.
