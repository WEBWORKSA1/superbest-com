Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate grinder?

Fresh, fine, consistent grinding matters more than the machine. Budget for a good burr grinder if the machine lacks one.

What is PID?

A digital temperature controller that keeps brew water stable for consistent shots.

How often should I descale?

Every 1–3 months depending on water hardness. Filtered water helps.
