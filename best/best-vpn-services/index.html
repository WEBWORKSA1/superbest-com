Frequently asked questions

Do I need a VPN?

It is useful on public Wi-Fi, for privacy from your ISP, and when travelling. It does not make you anonymous by itself.

Are free VPNs safe?

Most are not. Proton VPN's free tier is a rare trustworthy exception.

Will a VPN slow my internet?

Slightly. Top services using WireGuard-based protocols lose little speed on nearby servers.
